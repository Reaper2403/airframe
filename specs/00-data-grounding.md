## Anchor incident AF-104

Public aliases: Client C-004 and AP-04. Restricted local mapping for ingestion verification: client 3c:58:c2:00:00:04, BSSID 00:0b:86:04:00:00. Alias does not identify a worker or physical AP chassis; a BSSID is a logical BSS identifier. Multiple BSSIDs must not be merged into a physical AP without inventory evidence.

Raw anchors were independently decoded with Scapy, limiting inspected bytes to radiotap and the relevant management header/fixed parameters.

Human-readable reason-code labels require a verified protocol reference during implementation.
